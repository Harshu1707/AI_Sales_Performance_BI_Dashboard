# Dashboard Blueprint

## Page 1: Executive Overview

Purpose: Give leadership a fast read on business health.

Visuals:

- KPI cards: Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Sales Growth %, Average Order Value, Customer Count, Total Quantity Sold.
- Monthly Sales Trend Line Chart.
- 3-Month Sales Forecast Chart.
- Regional Sales Bar Chart.
- Category Sales Share Donut/Pie Chart.

Recommended filters:

- Order Date
- Region
- Segment
- Category

## Page 2: Regional Performance

Purpose: Identify high-performing and underperforming geographies.

Visuals:

- Filled map by state or country.
- Region-wise sales and profit clustered bar chart.
- State-wise profit ranking.
- Region profit margin table.

Drill-down:

- Country > Region > State > City.

## Page 3: Product Performance

Purpose: Understand product and category contribution.

Visuals:

- Category and sub-category revenue chart.
- Top 10 Products by Sales.
- Loss-making Products by Profit.
- Product profitability matrix.

Drill-down:

- Category > Sub-Category > Product Name.

## Page 4: Customer And Discount Insights

Purpose: Explain customer behavior and discount profitability.

Visuals:

- Customer Segment Analysis.
- Top Customers by Lifetime Sales.
- Profit vs Discount Scatter Plot.
- Discount Band Profitability Bar Chart.

Drill-down:

- Segment > Customer Name > Order ID.

## Interaction Design

- Use slicers for Date, Region, Segment, Category, and Sub-Category.
- Enable cross-filtering across all visuals.
- Add page navigation buttons for Executive, Region, Product, and Customer pages.
- Keep KPI cards fixed at the top of each page.
- Use tooltip pages for product and customer details.
